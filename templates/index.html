Clinical Neuromotor Science: Why Spiral & Wave Drawings Detect Parkinson's

1. Basal Ganglia & Dopamine Depletion

Parkinson's Disease originates from dopamine deficiency in the substantia nigra, damaging the basal ganglia-thalamocortical motor loop. This causes micro-tremors (4–7 Hz), muscle rigidity, and bradykinesia during fine motor tasks.

2. Archimedean Spiral Mechanics

Drawing an expanding Archimedean spiral requires continuous radius modulation and smooth acceleration. Patients with early-stage Parkinson's exhibit involuntary trajectory interruptions, micro-oscillations, and line compression.

3. Sine Wave Velocity Continuity

The sine wave test challenges rhythmic alternation between flexion and extension muscle groups. Tremors produce jagged local extrema and abrupt angular deviations visible in spatial and gradient frequency spectra.
